Paint Popper 🎈

Pick. Pump. POP. Repeat!

30 seconds. One shared canvas. A whole lot of paint chaos. Tap a spot, pump your paint balloon and pop it to splash your colour everywhere.

Bigger balloons make bigger splashes but take longer to pump. Newer paint covers older paint, so nobody's territory is safe. When the timer hits zero, the most visible paint wins!
